MR MONJUR Online Shop v33

Changes:
- Removed the large delivery-time panel from customer home page. Delivery estimate now appears in small text under each product.
- Home & Living, Electronics, Personal Care and Special Offers tiles are clickable filters.
- Admin can assign a category, delivery-time label, and Special Offer flag while creating/editing a product.

Install: replace online-shop.html, shop.html, hr.html and mr-logo-watermark.png in the repository root. Commit changes. In Admin Panel > Items & Stock, edit each product and assign its category. Set Special Offer? to Yes for offers.

Note: Existing products remain uncategorized until assigned in Admin Panel. Special Offers shows products with Special Offer? = Yes or category Special Offers.
